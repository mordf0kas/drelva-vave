Drelva Vave

Drelva Vave's UI and sound, as WebAssembly, on a drawn FM-1. Mouse, touch, or the computer keyboard (the letters on the keys). Nothing is saved; there is no USB. PLAY starts; FX to GLO pick tracks 1 to 6 (HOME held: mute); ARP is the master (delay, reverb, output), ARP again the system settings; OCT- / OCT+ change page, the last page of a track is MIX (KNOB 4 there loads another machine); KNOB 1 to 4 are the page; ALGORITHM is tempo; SEQ makes a new pattern or fires a hit; keys transpose; SAVE opens the presets (SELECT picks, SAVE stores, OCT+ loads, OCT- closes).
